How schools are grouped

Need adds up what is missing (classrooms, water, toilets, teachers, books...) and how many children are affected. Ready checks whether the school can receive and look after help: power, phone signal, a lockable room, truck access, someone to maintain it and a parents' association.

The weights can be changed in Settings. Each school's page explains its group in plain words.
